🌌 Who I Am

My work spans from organometallic chemistry and engineering to writing philosophical reflections and designing superhero tech. I’m driven by curiosity, guided by compassion, and endlessly hungry to learn.

Whether it's unlocking energy from biology, building smarter AI, or imagining the school of the future, everything I build is guided by a singular goal: to leave the world richer, kinder, and more curious than I found it.
